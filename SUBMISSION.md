# Dorm Room Booking — UI/UX Rescue Challenge Submission

## 1. Overview & Scenario
- **User Scenario:** A student needs to find available dates for a common dorm room, reserve it, and later adjust or cancel their own reservation.
- **Challenge Goal:** Identify at least 3 distinct, meaningful UI/UX problems in the baseline interface, propose alternatives, evaluate trade-offs, and implement functional, accessible solutions while preserving all existing behaviors, validations, and `localStorage` persistence.

---

## 2. Three Distinct UI/UX Problems & Justifications

### Problem 1: Misleading Calendar Slot Clicks, Low Visual Contrast & Broken Edit Flow
- **User's Goal:** Visually scan 18 rooms across the week to spot open slots, discern their own bookings vs. other students' bookings, and click to book or adjust without falling into interaction traps.
- **Why it hurt usability:**
  1. *Clickable Dead-ends:* Occupied/Booked cells were rendered as standard clickable buttons that populated the reservation form with taken dates. When the user attempted to confirm the booking, the system inevitably threw a conflict error.
  2. *Low Accessibility Contrast:* "Booked" (`#d9e7ed`) and "Open" (`#e5f7ef`) slots used nearly identical low-contrast pastel tones, making scanning across 126 cells exhausting and inaccessible.
  3. *Hidden Personal Bookings:* The student's own bookings had the exact same grey-blue background as other students' reservations, differing only by tiny text (`Your booking`), making personal bookings nearly impossible to spot at a glance.
  4. *Destructive Click Behavior During Edits:* When editing an existing reservation, clicking any calendar cell executed `setEditing(null)`, silently aborting edit mode and wiping the context without warning.
- **Solution Implemented:**
  - **High-contrast Visual Coding:** Vibrant accessible emerald green (`#ecfdf5` / `#047857`) for open slots, muted non-interactive slate for other students' reservations, and distinct indigo/purple (`#eef2ff` / `#4338ca`) with a prominent `★ You` badge for personal bookings.
  - **Context-Aware Slot Interaction:**
    - Clicking a student's own booking in the calendar enters edit mode for that reservation directly.
    - Clicking an open slot while in edit mode updates the reservation dates/room to the new target rather than aborting edit mode.
    - Disabled cursor with informative tooltip on other students' bookings to prevent dead-end clicks.
  - **Visual Edit Feedback:** Pulsating amber dashed border (`.slot.editing-slot`) on the calendar shows which room and dates are currently being modified.

---

### Problem 2: Obscured Validation Errors & Disconnected Availability Checking
- **User's Goal:** Confidently configure room reservation dates and understand immediately if a date range is valid and conflict-free without guesswork.
- **Why it hurt usability:**
  1. *Masked Error Messages:* `submit()` suppressed the specific validation details generated by `issue()` and threw a generic `Reservation failed. Review the selected dates.`, leaving the user clueless as to whether the conflict was an inverted date range, empty field, or overlapping booking.
  2. *Redundant Manual Checking Step:* Users were forced to click a separate "Check availability" button before submitting to verify if dates were open.
  3. *Unconstrained Native Date Inputs:* Date inputs lacked `min` constraints, allowing users to pick end dates earlier than start dates directly in the native datepicker.
  4. *Lack of Duration Transparency:* The functional brief notes that end dates are exclusive, but the form had no duration calculation (e.g., number of nights), leading to booking errors.
- **Solution Implemented:**
  - **Real-Time Live Status Pill:** Reactive status banner dynamically computes availability as the student types or changes dates (`✓ Available for X days`, `⚠ Conflict details`, or `ℹ Incomplete`).
  - **Specific, Actionable Error Messages:** When conflicts occur, the error message specifies the exact room number, dates, and whether it clashes with another student or an existing personal reservation.
  - **Smart Date Auto-Sync:** Selecting a start date automatically sets the end date to `startDate + 1 day` if unset or invalid, and sets `min={addDays(startDate, 1)}` on the end date picker.
  - **Duration Preview Badge:** Displays calculated nights and date clarification (e.g., "Selected duration: 2 nights (Nov 9 to morning of Nov 11)").

---

### Problem 3: Disconnected & Incomplete Reservation Management (Adjust & Cancel Flow)
- **User's Goal:** Review their reservations, adjust room or dates, and cancel/release rooms they no longer require.
- **Why it hurt usability:**
  1. *Disorienting Edit Trigger:* Clicking "Change" in the reservations list did not scroll or focus the form, leaving users on desktop and mobile unsure if the action registered.
  2. *Missing Cancellation Feature:* There was no way to release/delete a booking. If plans changed, rooms remained blocked indefinitely.
  3. *Navigation Disconnect:* The top navigation tab "My bookings" only performed a smooth scroll without managing view focus or displaying record counts.
- **Solution Implemented:**
  - **Seamless Edit Mode:** Clicking "Change" (or clicking your booking directly in the calendar) scrolls smoothly to the form, shows an "Editing Mode" banner at the top of the calendar, and highlights the active reservation card.
  - **Reservation Cancellation with Confirmation:** Added a "Cancel reservation" button to both the reservations list cards and the edit form, prompting the user with a confirmation dialog before releasing the room.
  - **Active Record Counters & Navigation:** Topbar navigation shows a dynamic badge with the count of active reservations (`My bookings [X]`).

## 3. Example of an AI Design Suggestion Refined / Rejected

- **Original AI Suggestion:**
  *Replace the weekly calendar table entirely with an Airbnb/hotel-style search filter interface (hide the calendar grid, show check-in / check-out input boxes, and list only available rooms as card results).*
- **Trade-off Analysis & Why Refined/Rejected:**
  - While an Airbnb-style search reduces visual density, it completely defeats the core functional brief: *"Review campus rooms, dates and existing reservations in one view across an example directory of 18 rooms."*
  - In a university dorm setting, students benefit from ambient awareness: seeing when peer study groups, clubs, and TA sessions are scheduled, and seeing which neighboring rooms are open.
  - Completely removing the calendar would eliminate the user's spatial mental model of the building wings.
- **Refinement Implemented:**
  - Preserved the full weekly matrix as the primary visual canvas, but introduced **lightweight, non-destructive cognitive aids**:
    1. Wing filter chips (`All wings`, `Wing A`, `Wing B`, `Wing C`, `Wing D`) to filter rooms in one click.
    2. Room search filter input (e.g. typing "20" or "B2").
    3. "Open dates only" toggle.
  - This solved cognitive overload while preserving the unified bird's-eye schedule view.

## 4. Demonstration of Preserved Functionality & Actions

1. **Main Action (Successful Booking):**
   - Click any open green slot on the calendar (e.g., Room `A1-203` on `11 Nov`).
   - The form autofills Room `A1-203`, Start date `2026-11-11`, End date `2026-11-12`, and displays live pill: `✓ Available for 1 day (11 Nov – 12 Nov)`.
   - Enter purpose "Team study session" and click "Confirm booking".
   - Notice displays: `"Room A1-203 successfully reserved for 11 Nov – 12 Nov."`, and the slot turns indigo with `★ You`.

2. **Unsuccessful Action (Conflict Prevention):**
   - Attempting to book Room `A1-203` for dates `2026-11-09` to `2026-11-11` (already booked by another student).
   - Live status pill turns red: `⚠ Conflict: Room A1-203 is already booked (9 Nov – 11 Nov) for another reservation.`
   - Attempting to submit alerts the exact error rather than generic text.

3. **Adjustment / Edit Action:**
   - Click "Change" on `A2-107` (or click the `★ You` cell on the calendar).
   - Banner displays: `"✏️ EDITING MODE: Adjusting reservation for A2-107"`.
   - Click open slot `A2-106` on `11 Nov`: target room and dates automatically update without canceling edit mode.
   - Click "Save booking changes": reservation updates in `localStorage`.
